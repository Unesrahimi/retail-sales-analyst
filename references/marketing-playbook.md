## 1. From finding to campaign

Every campaign should trace back to a finding:
`Finding (number) → Target (segment, count, file) → Offer → Channel → Timing → Budget ceiling → KPI and target`.

Example: "113 high-value one-time customers never came back → message the 108 with contact details with a time-limited gift card for their next purchase, in the first week of the next gift season → target: 8% return within 30 days."

Pick 3–5 campaigns. A small business won't execute twenty well.

## 2. Segment → campaign map

| Segment | Goal | Offer ideas | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| VIP | keep, raise advocacy | early access to a new collection, personal thank-you, a gift (not a discount), a private event | personal call/message, SMS, email |
| Loyal | more frequency, cross-sell | complementary product (from basket pairs), points/stamp card, referral reward | SMS/email, Instagram DM |
| At risk | win back before lost | "we miss you" plus a 14-day gift card | SMS/email, and a call for top spenders |
| Lost repeat | reactivate or learn why | stronger offer, a one-question survey | SMS/email |
| High-value one-timer | second purchase | personal thank-you, gift card or complementary-product offer with a deadline | SMS/email 30–60 days after purchase |
| New | second purchase | welcome, care tips, small incentive with a 30-day deadline | SMS/email 3–7 days after purchase |
| One-time cooling | reminder | new arrivals, seasonal message | newsletter, social |
| One-time dormant | cheap reactivation | only big occasions | bulk SMS/newsletter |
| Walk-ins / no contact | capture | small reward for joining the list, digital receipt, loyalty card | in-store, QR code |

Product-driven campaigns:
- **Rising stars / A items**: feature them in ads, the window and the homepage. Check stock covers the campaign.
- **Dead stock / overstock**: bundle with an A item, "second item −X%", gift-with-purchase, or an outlet corner. Prefer these to store-wide discounts.
- **Basket pairs**: an explicit bundle at a small saving, and staff suggesting the pair.
- **Declining A items**: check competitor prices, stock-outs and season before promoting.
- **Weak weekdays/hours**: a small targeted incentive for the slow day (e.g. a café's "Tuesday double stamps") instead of a general discount.

## 3. Offer design and discount guardrails

- Prefer **targeted, time-limited** offers to blanket discounts, which hand margin to people who would have bought anyway.
- Prefer **value adds** (gift, free accessory, gift card for the *next* purchase) to price cuts. They protect price perception and pull a second visit. A next-purchase voucher is the strongest tool against a low repeat rate.
- Cap discounts by margin. If the gross margin is unknown, ask before recommending more than 10–15%. Gift and discount amounts need the owner's approval.
- If peaks happen only in discount months, reduce the frequency of big discounts and attach them to occasions; customers have learned to wait.
- Use the `discounts` block: if discounted orders have a much lower AOV, set a minimum spend.

## 4. Iran

### Calendar (Shamsi)
Fixed:
- **Nowruz** (1 Farvardin). The biggest peak is the last 2–3 weeks of Esfand. Also Chaharshanbe Suri and Sizdah Bedar.
- **Mehr / back to school** (1 Mehr).
- **Black Friday «بلک فرایدی»** (late Aban / early Azar).
- **Yalda** (30 Azar), a gift night.
- **Valentine's** (25 Bahman) and **Sepandarmazgan** (29 Bahman).
- **Teacher's day** (12 Ordibehesht).

Moving (lunar), so verify the dates each year:
- **Mother's/Women's day** (20 Jumada al-Thani), a top gift occasion.
- **Father's/Men's day** (13 Rajab).
- **Ramadan, Muharram (Tasua/Ashura), Arbaeen, Fatimiyya**: avoid festive or loud promotions; use a respectful tone or pause.
- **Eid al-Fitr and Eid Ghadir**: gentle gift messaging is fine.

Typical peaks: Thursday/Friday and the start of the month. Check the weekday and day-of-month results rather than assuming.

### SMS rules (confirm with the user's SMS provider; rules change)
- A Persian (Unicode) SMS fits about 70 characters per part, and about 67 per part when concatenated. Keep campaigns to 1–2 parts.
- Advertising SMS normally needs an opt-out such as «لغو۱۱», and night-time sending is blocked.
- Personalize with the title and first name (خانم/آقای …), give one action and one deadline, and include the shop name and an address or link.

### Persian templates
- Second purchase: «[خانم/آقای نام] عزیز، ممنون که [برند] رو انتخاب کردید 🌿 یک کارت هدیه [مبلغ] تومانی برای خرید بعدی‌تون فعال شد؛ تا [تاریخ]. [آدرس/لینک] لغو۱۱»
- VIP early access: «[نام] عزیز، کالکشن جدید [برند] قبل از همه برای شما آماده‌ست. [تاریخ] تا [تاریخ] با یک هدیه ویژه منتظرتونیم. لغو۱۱»
- Win-back: «[نام] عزیز، دلمون براتون تنگ شده! تا [تاریخ] با ارائه این پیام [پیشنهاد] هدیه بگیرید. [برند] لغو۱۱»
- Occasion: «هدیه [مناسبت] رو از [برند] انتخاب کنید؛ بسته‌بندی هدیه رایگان + [پیشنهاد] تا [تاریخ]. [آدرس] لغو۱۱»
- Bundle: «[محصول A] + [محصول B] با [X]٪ تخفیف روی دومی، فقط تا [تاریخ]. [برند] لغو۱۱»

## 5. International (Europe, UK, US, elsewhere)

### Calendar: confirm the country, since dates and customs differ
Shared retail moments:
- **January sales**; **Valentine's day** (14 Feb).
- **Easter** (moving).
- **Mother's day**: UK and Ireland on Mothering Sunday in March; most of Europe and the US on the 2nd Sunday of May; France on the last Sunday of May (or early June).
- **Father's day**: mostly the 3rd Sunday of June; Germany on Ascension day; Spain and Italy on 19 March.
- **Summer sales** (late June to July); **back to school** (late August to September).
- **Halloween** (31 Oct); **Singles' Day** (11.11).
- **Black Friday** (the Friday after US Thanksgiving, i.e. late November) and **Cyber Monday**.
- **Advent and Christmas** (from late November; the biggest gift season); Boxing Day (UK, 26 Dec); New Year.

Country examples:
- **Czech Republic**: Mikuláš (5 Dec), Christmas markets, Christmas Eve gifts (24 Dec), MDŽ / International Women's Day (8 Mar), Easter Monday.
- **Germany/Austria**: Nikolaus (6 Dec), Christmas markets, Ascension-day Father's day.
- **Netherlands/Belgium**: Sinterklaas (5–6 Dec).
- **France/Italy/Spain**: regulated seasonal sales periods (soldes / saldi / rebajas); check the official dates.
- **US**: Memorial Day, 4th of July and Labor Day sales; Thanksgiving week.
- **Diaspora shops** (e.g. Iranian or Turkish shops abroad): also use their community's occasions (Nowruz, Yalda, Eid, Lunar New Year).

Typical peaks: **Saturday** (and Friday afternoon) for physical retail; weekday lunchtimes and mornings for cafés; Sunday evenings and Mondays for online; and around paydays (end/start of month). Check the data.

### Consent and pricing rules (EU/UK; summarize them, don't give legal advice)
- **GDPR + ePrivacy**: marketing email/SMS needs prior opt-in consent. Many countries allow a "soft opt-in" for existing customers about *similar* products, with an easy opt-out in every message. Phone numbers taken from invoices for delivery are **not** automatically marketable. Keep consent records, and filter `customers.csv` by consent before any send.
- Every marketing email or SMS needs the sender's identity and a working unsubscribe.
- **EU Omnibus directive**: when advertising a price reduction, the "before" price must be the lowest price of the previous 30 days. Fake "was" prices are illegal.
- **US**: CAN-SPAM for email (unsubscribe, physical address); SMS marketing needs express written consent (TCPA).

### Channels
Email newsletters (Mailchimp, Brevo, Klaviyo), SMS where customary, WhatsApp Business, Instagram/Facebook and TikTok, Google Business Profile (posts, and replies to reviews: a strong local lever), loyalty apps and stamp cards, local partnerships and events. Pick the channel the customers actually use; the contactability stats (`withPhonePct`, `withEmailPct`) show what's possible today.

### English templates (adapt the language and tone to the market)
- Second purchase (email subject + line): "A little thank-you from [Brand]". "Thanks for shopping with us, [First name]! Here's [€10] off your next visit, valid until [date]. [Link] · Unsubscribe"
- VIP early access: "[First name], you're first in line: our new [collection] is yours to preview from [date], with a small gift waiting in store."
- Win-back: "We miss you, [First name]! Come back by [date] and [offer]. [Brand] · [address]"
- Occasion: "Gifts for [occasion], sorted: free gift wrapping + [offer] until [date]."
- Bundle: "[Product A] + [Product B]: [X]% off the second one, this week only."

## 6. Measuring results

Decide these before sending: the target size; a 10–20% holdout that gets nothing (when the list is big enough); the response window (14–30 days); the KPIs (return rate of messaged vs holdout, revenue per message, code redemptions, unsubscribe rate). Re-run the analyzer after the window and compare.

When delivering a campaign list, build it from `customers.csv` (filtered by segment and contactability, and by consent for EU/UK). Add columns for the message, the send date and a sent? status. Flag rows that have `phoneCandidates` (ambiguous name matches) for manual checking.
